## Key facts / decisions
- Blender is Z-up metres; glTF export is Y-up: three (x, y, z) = Blender (x, z, -y). rooms.json/lights.json are already in three coords.
- Exported collections: everything except `00, 31b, 31c, 37, 38, 39b, 81, 85, 86, 91, 94` (alternate states, cameras, labels, section/overview helpers). Ceilings (90), ceiling fixtures (92) and skyline (95) ARE exported; viewer toggles them.
- Every exported mesh carries glTF extras → `userData`: `cat` (arch|floor|ceiling|ceilfix|exterior|furn), `rooms` ("A|B"), and for furniture `grp` (group id).
  Furniture groups = parts with same two-word name stem in same room whose bboxes touch (4 cm). Imperfect: plants/leaves and some TV-wall parts split or merge oddly.
- GOTCHA: multi-material Blender objects load in GLTFLoader as a Group (carrying the extras) + child meshes without extras. `init()` copies userData from the nearest tagged ancestor; without that, parts lose room/cat/group (skyline leaked into plan view, sofa parts stayed behind when moved).
- Room view has "Cutaway walls": arch pieces on the camera's side of the room are hidden each frame (`cutawayWalls()`), toggle `tCut`.
- Room isolation = hide objects whose `rooms` lacks the room + global clipping planes at room bounds ±0.25 m (so shared floors/walls are cut).
- Walk collision = 5 cm 2D occupancy grid built from all mesh triangles between z 0.25–1.7 m (excluding floors, ceilings, fixtures, skyline). Static: it does NOT update when furniture is moved in the editor. Player radius 0.2 m, eye 1.6 m. `C` toggles noclip.
- Rendering (v2): AgX tone mapping + custom grade ShaderPass approximating Blender's "AgX - Medium High Contrast" (Blender file: exposure -0.55, world warm grey 0.63/0.62/0.58 @0.7, EEVEE). Warm-tinted RoomEnvironment IBL, sun DirectionalLight with 4096 shadow map (autoUpdate off → call shadowsDirty() after edits/visibility), RectAreaLight per glazed opening facing into the room (daylight), GTAOPass ambient occlusion. Daylight/Evening + Shadows+AO toggles.
- Materials (v2): 41 Blender procedural materials baked by `tools/bake_materials.py` (Cycles, 1024 px, colour+roughness+normal, made tileable) → `tex/` + `materials.json`; applied tri-planar in metric object space via onBeforeCompile (`triplanar()`), 1 tile = 1.5 m. Glass converted from transmission to cheap transparent.
- Geometry (v2): `bakeAndMerge()` bakes every mesh transform into geometry (metric, needed for tri-planar) and merges per material: per furniture group (under its pivot) and per cat+rooms for floors/ceilings; arch pieces stay separate for cutaway. ~3990 → 1255 meshes.
- Clearances: selecting furniture ray-casts E/W/S/N at 3 heights × 3 offsets to the nearest wall/furniture; labels red under 0.60 m; `window.__app.clearances()`. URL `&sel=<name substring>` preselects, `&light=evening`, `&hq=0`, `&exp=`.
- Lighting (evening): Blender's 75 area lights are approximated by 10 three.js PointLights re-assigned to the nearest lights (fixed count avoids shader recompiles) + hemisphere light. Tune in `updateLights()` (intensity = min(30, 4+0.2W), hemisphere 0.45 walk / 1.0 plan, tone-map exposure 0.75).
- Editing: click furniture group → TransformControls on a pivot (move on floor plane snapped 5 cm, rotate Y snapped 15°). Delete, Shift+D duplicate, tint (recolours the group's dominant material only), Ctrl+Z undo. State auto-saves to localStorage key `candeur3302_layout_v1`; Save/Load layout = JSON file. Layout stores deltas keyed by group id, so ids must stay stable → re-export can change `g<N>` ids and orphan saved layouts.
- Ceiling height 3.05 m is an assumption (see parent README). Room sizes are printed floor-plan sizes.
